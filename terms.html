Bonito Shaving Experience

by Zen & Iki (藤田商店). By booking, you agree to these terms.

← Back to the experience

1. The experience

  • A hands-on, one-hour bonito (katsuobushi) shaving experience at your guesthouse or vacation rental: shave it, eat it, and taste the broth.
  • We bring all tools and ingredients, and we clean up afterwards.

2. Booking & price

  • Book through our Google Calendar booking page. Your booking is confirmed when we approve it and send you a confirmation email.
  • Price per person (transport included): Adult (18+) ¥3,000 · Student (6–17) ¥1,000 · Toddler (under 6) ¥500.
  • Minimum 2 adults. Students and toddlers do not count toward the minimum. Maximum 5 guests per session.
  • Pay in cash on the day.

3. Cancellation

  • Up to 24 hours before the start time: free.
  • Within 24 hours of the start time (including the same day): 50% of the total price.
  • No-show: 50% of the total price.
  • We run rain or shine. The experience is indoors.
  • If we have to cancel (illness, natural disaster, transport disruption, etc.), you pay nothing and we will help you find another time.

4. Safety

  • The shaving box has a blade. Please follow our instructions.
  • We provide new cut-resistant gloves and a holder for every guest. Please wear the gloves.
  • Guests under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian who stays with them at all times.
  • Toddlers (under 6) take part under the direct supervision of a guardian. For safety, we may ask that they not handle the blade.
  • We may stop the experience if we judge it unsafe (for example, due to alcohol) or if instructions are not followed.

5. Food allergies & dietary needs

  • Main ingredients: bonito (fish), soy sauce (soy, wheat), shichimi spice blend (may contain sesame and others), plain rice, and cold tofu (soy).
  • Please tell us about allergies before you book. The experience is based on fish (bonito), so it is not suitable for vegetarians or vegans.

6. Insurance & injuries

  • We carry liability insurance covering our legal liability for accidents during the experience (FREENANCE "Anshin Hosho", underwritten by Sompo Japan).
  • We do not provide insurance for your own medical costs. Please check that your travel insurance covers activities like this.
  • You take part at your own responsibility, except for injury or damage caused by our intentional misconduct or gross negligence, or where the law makes us liable.
  • If an accident or injury happens, please tell us right away.

7. Photos & social media

  • You are welcome to film and post (please be considerate of other guests and host staff who appear in your shots).
  • We may photograph or film the experience for promotion (social media, website). If you prefer not to appear, tell us before we start.

8. Your accommodation

  • The host kindly lends us the space. Please follow the house rules and take care of the property.
  • If a guest damages the host's property, we may ask that guest to take responsibility for it.

9. Personal information

  • We use the information you give when booking only to run the experience and contact you. We do not share it with third parties.
