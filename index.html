Safety & good to know

Fun, and safe.

🧤 Safety first

The shaving box has a blade, so we provide new cut-resistant gloves for every guest at no charge. Guests under 18 stay with a parent or guardian at all times.

🛡️ Insured

We carry liability insurance for the experience. It does not cover your own medical costs, so please check that your travel insurance covers activities like this.

🍚 Food & allergies

Contains fish (bonito), soy and wheat. The spice blend may contain sesame. Not suitable for vegetarians or vegans. Please tell us about allergies before you book.

FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need any experience?

No. We show you how, and gloves are provided. If you can pull a block across a blade, you can do it.

Can kids join?

Yes. Students (6–17) are ¥1,000 and toddlers (under 6) are ¥500. Anyone under 18 must stay with a parent or guardian. Toddlers take part with a guardian right beside them, and we may ask that they not handle the blade.

I'm vegetarian / vegan. Can I join?

Sorry, no. The experience is built around bonito (fish), so it is not suitable for vegetarians or vegans.

What if it rains?

Nothing changes. The experience is indoors and runs rain or shine.

How do I pay?

In cash on the day. Prices are per person and include transport.

Can I film and post it?

Please do. We may also film the session for our own promotion. If you'd rather not appear, just tell us before we start.

How many people can join?

At least 2 adults, and up to 5 guests in total per session.
